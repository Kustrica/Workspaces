Firefox Sync

Workspaces and tabs sync automatically across your devices. No manual action is needed.

Sign into the same Mozilla account on each device and check that Add-ons sync is enabled. To sync now, open the Firefox account menu at the top right → Sync → Sync Now.

When you add a new device, turn Sync on there. Only that new device asks once: download the synced copy or merge with what is already open.
